How ordered pairs work

(x, y) gives a point’s location. Start at the origin, (0, 0). For x, positive means right and negative means left. For y, positive means up and negative means down. A zero means no movement in that direction.

Example: (−3, 2) means 3 left, then 2 up. Order matters! On a keyboard, focus the graph and use arrow keys; press Enter to check. You can also use the labeled + and − buttons.
